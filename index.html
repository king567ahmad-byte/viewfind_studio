Everything you need to know about booking View Find Studio in Lahore:

We recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance, especially for peak winter wedding dates in Lahore (November through March). Because we accept a limited number of weddings per season to maintain editorial quality, dates are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis upon deposit.
Our packages include complete senior team coverage (candid stills + cinema video), full-event drone aerial cinematography, high-resolution master-edited photographs, a cinematic wedding teaser, full film edits, and luxury handmade signature photobooks.
You receive a teaser preview within 48–72 hours after the event for sharing with family and social media. The complete batch of color-graded high-resolution photos and final cinematic films are delivered within 3 to 4 weeks.
