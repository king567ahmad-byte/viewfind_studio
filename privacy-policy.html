Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Effective for all clients of View Find Studio (Lahore, Pakistan).

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry or book a photography shoot with View Find Studio, we may collect the following details:

  • Your name and contact phone / WhatsApp number.
  • Event dates, event venue (e.g., Lahore, Islamabad, destination) and shooting requirements.
  • Package preferences selected in our website pricing estimator.

2. Client Photo Privacy & Confidentiality

We respect family, cultural, and personal privacy. We understand that certain wedding ceremonies (especially bridal portraits or private family gatherings) require strict discretion:

  • No Unauthorized Public Posting: If you request complete privacy for your wedding or family photographs, we will strictly honor that agreement and will not publish your photos or films on our public website or social media channels without your explicit consent.
  • Secure Storage: All raw and edited high-resolution media is preserved on secure studio hard drives and encrypted cloud storage for your archival peace of mind.

3. Analytics & Cookies

Our website may use privacy-compliant measurement cookies (such as Google Analytics 4) to understand which portfolio projects and packages are most viewed by visitors. We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data to third-party advertising brokers.
